Programs

Community Partnerships

Community Connect builds bridges between Westminster and local organizations, neighbors, and community leaders. Through ongoing partnerships, students learn that lasting impact is built on trust, relationship, and a shared commitment to the well-being of others. Outreach  ·  Relationships  ·  Lasting Impact

Thanksgiving Food Drive

Students and families come together each year to provide Thanksgiving meals for families in need through the Angel Share Food Drive. In partnership with Laura Saunders K-8 Center in Florida City, Westminster collects food donations that are assembled into Thanksgiving meal baskets and distributed to families identified by the school's support staff. Through this annual tradition of generosity, students learn the importance of compassion, gratitude, and caring for their neighbors during the holiday season.

Community Impact

Since 2010, Westminster families have helped provide Thanksgiving meals to hundreds of families in Florida City, demonstrating how a caring community can make a meaningful difference in the lives of others.

Summer Kickoff

Students participate in a summer service initiative supporting Youth for Christ's KIX Summer programming by collecting books and noisy offering to help provide resources for students in need. Through this annual effort, students learn the value of generosity, literacy advocacy, and supporting organizations that serve youth in the broader community. This initiative helps students see how their actions can contribute to meaningful summer experiences for others.

Community Impact

Since 2010, Westminster students and families have consistently shown up to expand summer opportunities for youth in the Homestead community proving that small acts of generosity, when united, create lasting change.

Family Engagement

Families are encouraged to participate alongside students in service opportunities and celebrations of giving. Partnership  ·  Belonging  ·  Shared Purpose

Global Literacy

Through curated learning experiences, students explore the needs of communities near and far. Global literacy helps students understand their role as citizens of the world and as ambassadors of Christ's love equipped to respond with compassion and intentional action. Global Awareness  ·  Citizenship  ·  Compassion

International Festival Day

Students explore global cultures, traditions, and communities through an interactive, school-wide experience that brings learning to life. Through food, music, language, storytelling, and cultural displays, students gain a deeper understanding of the diverse world God has created and their role as global citizens. Families are invited to participate alongside students, strengthening connections between home, school, and the wider world while celebrating shared humanity and Christ-centered compassion.

Gratitude Practices

Regular gratitude practices woven into the school year help students cultivate thankful hearts and recognize the blessings around them. These practices shape a posture of generosity, contentment, and joy foundational to a life of faithful service. Gratitude  ·  Generosity  ·  Joyful Living

Noisy Offering

Students learn the joy of generosity by participating in quarterly Noisy Offerings that support local, national, and global organizations. Throughout the year, students bring coins and bills to "make some noise" while contributing to causes that serve others and meet real needs. Through these giving opportunities, students discover that everyone can make a difference and that even small gifts can have a meaningful impact when combined with the efforts of a caring community.
