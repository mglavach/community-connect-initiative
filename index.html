Community Connect Initiative

Community Connect equips students to become Christ-centered servants who build meaningful relationships, recognize the needs of others, and make a positive impact in their communities. Through service learning, family engagement, mentorship, global literacy, gratitude practices, and community partnerships, students learn that leadership begins with serving others.

Why Community Connect?

  1. Cultivates Gratitude, Empathy, and Compassion — Through meaningful service experiences that shape students' hearts and perspectives.
  2. Encourages Positive Impact — Students recognize how their actions and choices can make a real difference in the lives of others.
  3. Connects Learning to Life — Classroom experiences are linked to real-world opportunities for service and stewardship.
  4. Strengthens Relationships — Builds meaningful connections among students, families, schools, and community partners.
  5. Develops Global Awareness — Fosters an understanding of responsible citizenship both locally and around the world.
  6. Provides Mentorship & Leadership Opportunities — Students grow through collaboration, service, and opportunities to lead with purpose.
  7. Helps Students Discover Their Gifts — Empowers students to identify and use their God-given talents in service to others.
  8. Fosters a Lifelong Commitment to Community — Instills a lasting dedication to contributing with purpose and integrity.
  9. Reinforces Westminster's Mission — Develops Christ-centered leaders who influence the world for Christ by reflecting God's love through purposeful action.
  10. Transforms Service into a Mindset — Moves service from an activity students do into a mindset they carry throughout their lives.

Programs

Service Learning

Students engage in purposeful, structured service projects that connect classroom learning to real community needs. Through reflection and action, students develop empathy, responsibility, and an understanding of how service can transform both the giver and the recipient. Empathy  ·  Responsibility  ·  Stewardship

Nature Ambassador

Students explore God's creation, develop an appreciation for the natural world, and inspire others to care for the environment through stewardship and service. Through learning and leadership opportunities, students become advocates for protecting and preserving the world God has entrusted to us.

Global Literacy

Through curated learning experiences, students explore the needs of communities near and far. Global Awareness  ·  Citizenship  ·  Compassion

Noisy Offering

Students learn the joy of generosity by participating in quarterly Noisy Offerings that support local, national, and global organizations. Throughout the year, students bring coins and bills to "make some noise" while contributing to causes that serve others and meet real needs. Through these giving opportunities, students discover that everyone can make a difference and that even small gifts can have a meaningful impact when combined with the efforts of a caring community.

Thanksgiving Food Drive

Students and families come together each year to provide Thanksgiving meals for families in need through the Angel Share Food Drive. In partnership with Laura Saunders K-8 Center in Florida City, Westminster collects food donations that are assembled into Thanksgiving meal baskets and distributed to families identified by the school's support staff. Through this annual tradition of generosity, students learn the importance of compassion, gratitude, and caring for their neighbors during the holiday season.

Community Impact

Since 2010, Westminster families have helped provide Thanksgiving meals to hundreds of families in Florida City, demonstrating how a caring community can make a meaningful difference in the lives of others.

KIX Summer Kickoff for Youth for Christ

Students participate in a summer service initiative supporting Youth for Christ's KIX Summer programming by collecting books and noisy offering to help provide resources for students in need. Through this annual effort, students learn the value of generosity, literacy advocacy, and supporting organizations that serve youth in the broader community. This initiative helps students see how their actions can contribute to meaningful summer experiences for others.

Community Impact

Since 2010, Westminster students and families have consistently shown up to expand summer opportunities for youth in the broader community — proof that small acts of generosity, when united, create lasting change.
